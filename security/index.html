Security, Cookie & Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date

June 7, 2026

Section 1 — Security Overview

FinFavorite takes reasonable measures designed to protect customer information.

Security measures may include:

  • Encryption in transit;
  • Encryption at rest where appropriate;
  • Access controls;
  • Authentication safeguards;
  • Monitoring and logging systems;
  • Vendor security reviews.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. FinFavorite does not guarantee that unauthorized access, security breaches, or cyberattacks will never occur. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of account credentials.

Responsible Disclosure

Security concerns may be reported to security@finfavorite.com.

Section 2 — Cookie Policy

FinFavorite may use cookies, local storage technologies, and similar technologies to:

  • Maintain user sessions;
  • Authenticate users;
  • Remember preferences;
  • Analyze Service usage;
  • Improve platform performance;
  • Detect abuse and fraud.

Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies. Required for operation of the Services.

Analytics Cookies. Used to understand how users interact with the Services.

Preference Cookies. Used to remember user settings and preferences.

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow users to manage or disable cookies. Disabling cookies may impact Service functionality.

Section 3 — Data Retention

FinFavorite retains information only as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide Services;
  • Maintain account functionality;
  • Comply with legal obligations;
  • Resolve disputes;
  • Protect platform integrity.

Retention periods may vary based on data type and applicable legal requirements.

Section 4 — Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account at any time. Deletion requests may be submitted through privacy@finfavorite.com, or through account settings where available.

Upon receipt of a verified deletion request:

  • User account access will be terminated;
  • Personal information will be scheduled for deletion;
  • Connected account access will be revoked where applicable.

Backup Systems

Certain information may remain in secure backups for a limited period following deletion before permanent removal.

Legal Retention

FinFavorite may retain information where required by law, regulation, court order, legal process, fraud prevention requirements, or legitimate business interests.

Verification

FinFavorite may require verification of identity before fulfilling deletion requests.

Response Time

FinFavorite will make reasonable efforts to process verified deletion requests within thirty (30) days, subject to applicable law and operational constraints.

Contact

Questions regarding security, cookies, retention, or deletion may be directed to privacy@finfavorite.com or security@finfavorite.com.
