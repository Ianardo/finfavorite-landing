About FinFavorite

We built FinFavorite because we couldn't find it anywhere else.

We're self-directed investors who run multiple strategies — long-term holds, active trades, income plays — and nothing we tried could show us how they all worked together. Everything either did one thing well or tried to do everything and became unusable. None of it answered the question we actually cared about: is what I'm doing working?

Not "what's my balance." More like: is my trading actually helping my returns, or just keeping me busy? Is the time I spend on a strategy worth what it gives back? How does everything fit together?

So we built it ourselves.

FinFavorite is a single workspace where your strategies, accounts, and assets live side by side — so you can see how each one contributes to the whole picture. It's meant to be powerful without being a chore to use.

We're two founders and a small team. The product is evolving fast and we pay attention to every piece of feedback we get. If you're using FinFavorite right now, you're not just along for the ride — you're helping shape what it becomes.

If you're the kind of investor who doesn't fit neatly into one category, this is what we're building for.
