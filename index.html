Can FinFavorite see my broker credentials?

  • No. Broker connections use secure third-party integrations or exports you control.
  • FinFavorite never needs your brokerage password to place trades or move money.
  • If you prefer not to connect or sync your accounts at all, you can use broker exports instead.

Can FinFavorite move my money?

  • No. FinFavorite is built for tracking, analysis, and review, not for custody or money movement.
  • We never transfer funds or take custody of assets.
  • Your accounts remain at your broker, exchange, bank, or institution.

Can FinFavorite place trades?

  • No. FinFavorite separates analysis from execution.
  • We do not directly execute trades or rebalance accounts.
  • Investing and trading actions happen through your broker.
